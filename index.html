Capability Building

Hands-On Training Sessions

Practical, industry-rooted workshops delivered on-site, in universities, or remotely equipping teams, suppliers and students with the knowledge to compete at global standard.

Workshop Module 01

Seasonal Print & Embroidery Masterclass

A focused programme on reading and translating seasonal trends into viable print and embroidery directions; covering colour palettes, repeat systems, digital vs. screen print decisions, and buyer brief interpretation.

  • Trend sourcing from global trade fairs (ITMA, FESPA, Denim Expo)
  • Translating macro trends into seasonal collection narratives
  • Print costing, benchmarking, and vendor briefing
  • Embroidery specification writing and sampling quality checks
Workshop Module 02

Sustainability & Compliance Essentials

Helping factory teams understand and navigate the environmental standards that global brands demand from chemical compliance, Aiding A-Z in traceability and responsible manufacturing practices.

  • ZDHC MRSL and REACH chemical compliance fundamentals
  • Higg FEM environmental module walkthrough
  • ETP setup, wastewater management, and audit readiness
  • SDG alignment, Decent Work, Innovation and Responsible Production
Workshop Module 03

Lean Manufacturing & Zero-Defect Culture

Building a production floor mindset of quality-first, waste reduction, and continuous improvement. Aligned with frameworks applied across top companies such as H&M's global supplier network.

  • Value stream mapping and bottleneck identification
  • AQL 2.5 quality systems and first-sample approval protocols
  • Rejection root cause analysis and corrective action tools
  • KPI dashboard setup and performance tracking cadence
Workshop Module 04

Digital Tools & PLM Traceability

Equipping supplier and brand teams with confidence in digital workflows. Ranging from PLM–IMQ integration for full order traceability to Excel-based forecasting models and data-driven decision-making.

  • PLM system navigation and IMQ integration best practices
  • Digital sampling workflows and approval processes
  • Excel forecasting models and KPI scorecards (live build)
  • CriticalPath PO management and change request handling
